Privacy policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy explains what information Yakshvi Studios ("we", "us") handles on this website and in our apps and games, and why. We keep it in plain language and update it whenever something changes.

This website

  • We don't use cookies, analytics, advertising trackers or sign-up forms on this website.
  • Our fonts and images are served from this website itself, so visiting it doesn't send your information to font or image services.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may record visitors' IP addresses for security and to keep the service running. You can read about this in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Our apps and games

Each app or game we publish will have its own section on this page before it's released. That section will set out, for that app:

  • what information it collects, if any;
  • why it's needed and how it's used;
  • whether any of it is shared with third parties, such as app stores, advertising or analytics services;
  • how long it's kept, and how you can ask us to delete it.

We haven't released any apps yet, so there are no app sections here yet.

When you email us

If you write to us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to reply to you, and we don't add you to mailing lists.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing us. We'll respond as soon as we reasonably can.

Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Significant changes for an app will also be noted in that app's section.
