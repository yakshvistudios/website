Notice where your money goes.

Note what you spend, by hand. Small amounts add up, and writing them down is how you start to notice them. An aana was a sixteenth of a rupee: Aanote is for the small amounts as much as the big ones.

  • A one-time purchase, no subscription

Note it in two taps

Type the amount, tap what it was for, and it's saved. No forms, no save button. The keypad is a calculator too, for splitting a bill before you save it.

Account, date and a short note are there when you need them. Hold the app's icon for a shortcut straight to a new entry.

Budgets that nudge, not nag

Set a monthly limit for the categories that matter to you. Each shows how much is left, and warns you from 80%, before it's too late, not after.

Reminders are your choice too: off, once a day, at meal times, or every two hours, and they stay quiet when you've just added something.

Cash, bank and cards

Keep track of what's in each account. Add money to your wallet after a trip to the ATM, and see how much cash went through in a month.

Credit cards show what you owe and what's still available on your limit. Paying the bill isn't counted as spending twice: the purchases already were.

Private by design

Your records live only on your phone, in an encrypted database. There's no account to create and no server of ours holding a copy. Aanote doesn't read your SMS or connect to your bank.

Backups are yours to turn on: to your own Google Drive or a file you keep, encrypted on your phone with a passphrase only you know. App lock uses your phone's own fingerprint, face or PIN. And Delete all my data really deletes it.

Help and questions

How much does it cost?

Aanote is a one-time purchase on Google Play. There are no ads, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Refunds are handled by Google Play's refund process.

How do I back up my records?

Go to Settings, then Backup and restore. You can back up to your own Google Drive or to a file you save. You choose a passphrase, and the backup is encrypted on your phone with it before it goes anywhere. You also get a recovery key: keep it somewhere safe. Backups to Drive can run by themselves, daily, weekly or monthly.

How do I move to a new phone?

Back up on the old phone first. Then install Aanote on the new one, go to Settings, then Backup and restore, and restore from Google Drive or from your backup file. You'll need your passphrase or your recovery key.

I forgot my backup passphrase.

Use your recovery key instead: it opens the backup on its own. If both the passphrase and the recovery key are lost, the backup can't be opened by anyone, including us, because we never receive either of them.

Does Aanote read my SMS or connect to my bank?

No. You note what you spend yourself. That's the point of the app: writing it down is how you notice it. Aanote never asks for your SMS, contacts or bank details.

What currencies does it support?

Each account can be in Indian rupees, US dollars, euros or British pounds.

How do I delete everything?

Go to Settings, then Delete all my data. You don't need to delete your Google Drive backup separately: if Drive is connected, Aanote deletes the backup for you as part of the same step, once you've confirmed, and then erases your records on the phone. If the backup can't be deleted (for example, with no internet), nothing is erased, and the app tells you.

To keep your Drive backup, disconnect Google in Backup and restore before deleting. To delete only the Drive backup, go to Backup and restore, then Delete my Drive backup.

Which phones does it work on?

Android phones running Android 7.0 or later. It isn't available for iPhone yet.
